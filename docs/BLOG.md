# Blog Page post

**Title:** Rihla: a Bahrain trip planner that explains its own decisions

For the Session 16 task I used Claude to build **Rihla** (رحلة, "journey"), a web app that plans a trip around Bahrain. You describe the trip in your own words, for example "two days, history and seafood, student budget". Rihla shows which words it understood and turns them into settings you can check. It then builds a timed itinerary across 30 real places, from Qal'at al-Bahrain to the Pearling Path in Muharraq, and draws it on a live map. Unlike a simple list app, it works out opening hours, closing days, drive times and meal stops, and it keeps each day in one part of the island. What links it to AI Literacy is that it never hides its reasoning. Every stop has a "Why this stop" explanation, and a "How Rihla made this plan" panel shows the inputs, the rules, the places it left out and why, and the things it cannot know, such as live traffic or today's exact hours. You can swap, reorder or remove stops, save the trip, share it as a link, or add it to your calendar.

- Live app: https://potatos-119.github.io/rihla/
